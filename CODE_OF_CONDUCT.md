# Code of conduct

Shiro exists to help people through a stressful part of their lives: looking for work. Its community should feel the same way.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source, to Claude Code or to job hunting.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's resume, contact details or application data.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sharing other people's personal information.
- Spam, or promoting tools that fabricate credentials or bypass hiring sites' terms.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@priyansh1210](https://github.com/priyansh1210)).
